A shop selling one-off consultations today may add a six-session coaching pack next month and a group workshop after that. ### The advanced details worth knowing

- **Repeat visits:** offer booking bundles and recurring service subscriptions rather than treating every appointment as a separate sale.
- **Real availability:** work with service, teammate or equipment availability and participant categories.
- **Connected operations:** the beta public API can read services and bookings, create a manual booking and attach it to a subscription credit.
- **Useful events:** beta webhooks include attendance changes and failed subscription billing, alongside booking lifecycle events. Signed payloads and delivery retries are documented.

**What would you pay?** At 20 bookings × $40, monthly booking revenue is $800: Starter is $13.99. At $2,000, Basic is $29.99. At $4,000, Growth is $59.99. The listed ceilings are $1,000, $3,000 and $10,000 respectively. Optional messaging charges and other applicable costs remain separate.

**Check before choosing:** the API is beta. API-created bookings do not create a Shopify order, and the current API does not offer available-slot lookup or booking update/delete endpoints. Use the [API and webhook documentation](https://help.cowlendar.com/how-to-integrations/connect-cowlendar-to-crm-erp-or-app-public-api-webhooks) to validate a custom integration.

## How to choose the right Shopify booking app

**Write the workflow before choosing the plan.** List the services, staff, equipment, booking formats and payment rules you need. Specify whether a calendar connection must import busy time, export appointments or do both. Decide whether recurring customers buy separate sessions, a prepaid pack or a renewing subscription.

**Price a normal month and a busy month.** The [comparison directory](/compare) includes calculators for ten apps, with booking volume, average value and feature requirements. Keep app subscriptions separate from Shopify costs, payment processing, messaging and extra staff access. Use the [service price calculator](/free-tools/service-price-calculator) to check your own service economics.

**Test the awkward booking.** Fill the last seat, create an external calendar conflict, move an appointment and cancel a paid reservation. Confirm which emails fire and how the booking, order and refund records change. For an API integration, inspect the actual endpoints and event payloads.

**Make the schedule realistic.** Use our [time slot generator](/free-tools/time-slot-generator) to plan service duration and buffer time. Use the [no-show calculator](/free-tools/no-show-calculator) to put numbers on missed visits, without assuming that any app guarantees a particular improvement.
