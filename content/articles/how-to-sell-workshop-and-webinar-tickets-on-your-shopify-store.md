## Choose a session-based product
Create a clear event offer with its topic, format, duration, audience and included materials. For a live webinar, show the time zone and explain whether a recording will be available. For a workshop, include the venue and practical arrival details.

Decide whether customers buy one ticket per attendee or one booking for a whole group. The booking configuration must reflect that choice so remaining capacity stays accurate.

## Connect capacity to the event date
A product can sell on several dates, but each session needs its own available places. Check that a sale for Friday does not reduce Saturday's capacity unless they genuinely share inventory.

If the instructor or venue is shared with another service, model that resource too. An unlimited webinar audience does not mean the presenter can host two live events at once.

## Keep meeting access tied to the booking
Cowlendar lists Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams integrations. Other booking apps support different meeting providers and plan requirements. Confirm whether a link is generated per appointment, per session or manually, and whether multiple participants receive the correct shared session link.

Do not publish private meeting credentials in the product description. Test the booking confirmation from a customer's perspective, including the message received after a date change. Check the meeting provider's participant limits and settings separately from Shopify's order flow.

| Stage | Customer should receive | Staff should verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase | Event, date, time zone and payment summary | Correct session and participant count |
| Confirmation | Access instructions or venue details | Working link and intended audience |
| Reminder | Clear joining or arrival action | Correct timing for the attendee's zone |
| Change | Updated details and policy outcome | Old slot released, new slot reserved |
| Follow-up | Promised materials or recording instructions | Access only as intended |

## Decide how ticket changes work
Can the purchaser replace an attendee? Can one person in a group cancel? Is the ticket transferable? Set the policy before launch and verify the app can represent the corresponding changes.

A refund and a canceled reservation may require separate actions. Check both, especially when only part of a multi-person purchase changes. For paid recordings or follow-up access, confirm whether cancellation should revoke that access.

## Test from a phone and a second email address
Complete a booking in a private browser window using an address outside your staff account. Open the confirmation on a phone, follow the joining instructions, then reschedule or cancel the test reservation.

For a webinar, run a rehearsal with the meeting host and one attendee. Check that the host knows where participant records live and what to do if someone has paid but cannot find the email.

After the event, compare purchased seats, confirmed attendees and actual attendance. These are different measures. Use the differences to improve reminders and instructions rather than reporting order count as attendance.
