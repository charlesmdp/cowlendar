## What does the Built for Shopify badge actually mean?

Built for Shopify is Shopify's own quality certification, not a marketing claim from the app developer. To earn it, an app has to pass Shopify's review on three fronts: performance (it can't slow your storefront down by more than 10 points), design (it follows Shopify's admin standards), and integration (it uses Shopify's current technology, not a legacy workaround).

That matters more for booking apps than for most other app categories. A booking widget has to check real-time availability, block double bookings, and sync a calendar, all before a customer finishes loading your product page. An app that drags your storefront down doesn't just look bad. It costs you bookings from customers who give up and close the tab.

Shopify doesn't hand this out for free. Apps apply for it, and Shopify tests the app's real impact on a live store before granting it. That's different from a star rating, which measures how happy merchants are after they've already installed the thing. Built for Shopify measures whether the app is technically sound before you ever click install.

BookX, built by Logbase, holds 603 reviews at 5.0 stars and carries the Built for Shopify badge. Built by Servicify and holding 537 reviews at 4.9 stars, Easy Appointment Booking App is Built for Shopify certified and leans hardest into events: tours, tastings, workshops, and group sessions with QR ticket check-in built in.

If your booking business looks more like an experience (a wine tasting, a walking tour, a pottery class) than a one-on-one appointment, the QR ticketing and multi-location scheduling here are built specifically for that. ## FAQ

### What does the Built for Shopify badge mean?

It's Shopify's highest certification for third-party apps. To earn it, an app has to pass Shopify's own testing on performance (it can't slow your storefront by more than 10 points), design (it follows Shopify's admin standards), and integration (it uses Shopify's current technology stack). Shopify grants it after direct testing, not based on merchant reviews alone.

### How many booking apps have the Built for Shopify badge?

As of September 2026, five booking apps with a substantial review history carry the badge: Cowlendar, Apntly, Appointo, BookX, and Easy Appointment Booking App. Sesami and Tipo also hold the certification but have fewer reviews than the five featured here. Newer apps without an established review base may be certified too but are harder to evaluate for reliability.

### Is BookThatApp Built for Shopify certified?

No. BookThatApp has been on the Shopify App Store since 2010 and holds around 500 reviews at 4.7 stars, but it has not earned Built for Shopify certification. That doesn't disqualify it as a tool, but it means it hasn't passed Shopify's own performance and integration review the way the five apps in this list have.

### Does a Built for Shopify badge guarantee an app is the best fit for my store?

No. The badge confirms an app meets Shopify's technical bar for speed, design, and integration. It doesn't tell you whether the app covers the specific booking type your business needs, whether its pricing model fits your volume, or whether its feature set matches your workflow. Use the badge as a trust filter, then compare features against your actual use case.

### Can I filter the Shopify App Store by the Built for Shopify badge?

Yes. Browse or search a category on the Shopify App Store, then use the Built for Shopify filter option to show only certified apps. You can also ask Shopify's Sidekick assistant directly for Built for Shopify recommendations in your category.
