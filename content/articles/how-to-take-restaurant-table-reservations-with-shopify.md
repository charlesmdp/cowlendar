## Choose between time slots and table management
A small venue offering a few fixed dinner sittings may only need reservations against a defined capacity. A busy restaurant with walk-ins, table combinations, staggered arrivals and variable dining times needs more detailed operational control.

Decide whether a booking reserves a seat, a whole table or a party within a service window. Do not multiply a simple appointment capacity by party size and assume the floor plan will work.

## Define each service window
Set lunch and dinner availability separately, including closed dates and private events. Specify the expected seating duration and the time needed before the next party arrives. Keep kitchen and staff capacity in mind: an empty table does not necessarily mean the restaurant can accept another large party at that moment.

For an illustrative 12:00–14:00 seating, test an arrival near the end of the window. The system should either enforce a latest arrival or clearly communicate the available dining time.

## Collect the minimum useful information
Ask for the reservation name, party size and a contact route. Offer a way to discuss accessibility or dietary needs privately where appropriate. Avoid promising that a free-text request is automatically accepted; explain when the team must confirm it.

| Requirement | Question to verify with the app |
| --- | --- |
| Party size | Does each guest reduce the correct capacity? |
| Table allocation | Can specific tables or table combinations be reserved? |
| Large groups | Can staff review a request before confirmation? |
| Seating turnover | Do duration and buffers protect the next reservation? |
| Deposits | How are payment, cancellation and refund linked? |

## Explain any reservation payment
If you collect a deposit or prepayment, show the amount and its purpose before the customer books. Clarify whether it is credited against the bill, the applicable cancellation policy and the process for changes. Payment rules vary by provider and jurisdiction, so validate the policy for your business rather than copying another restaurant's wording.

A booking deposit feature does not automatically create a card-on-file no-show charging workflow. Ask the vendor and payment provider about the specific action you intend to perform.

## Test the host's workflow
Make several reservations with different party sizes. Move one party, cancel another and simulate a walk-in. Verify the team can see the current service without reconciling contradictory lists.

If the app cannot represent the floor plan or real-time seating decisions you need, use a dedicated restaurant reservation system and connect Shopify for relevant sales. Choosing a specialist tool can be the better operational decision.

Cowlendar provides appointment and group-booking building blocks. That makes it worth evaluating for structured reservation offers, but it should not be presented as a complete restaurant point-of-sale or table-management platform.
