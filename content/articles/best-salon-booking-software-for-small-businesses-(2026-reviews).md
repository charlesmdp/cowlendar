## FAQ

### Can I track the schedules of multiple stylists on a single booking calendar?

Yes, you can. Modern booking apps allow you to add multiple team members, set their individual working hours, and assign them to specific services. When a client visits your booking widget, they can pick their preferred stylist or select the first available professional.

### How do I prevent clients from booking appointment slots that I have blocked out?

When you link your booking software with your Google Calendar or Outlook, your availability updates in real time. Any personal events, vacation days, or staff meetings created on your personal calendar will block out those slots on your store, keeping your calendar safe.

### Is it possible to sell retail products alongside salon appointment bookings?

Yes. When you use a native booking app inside your Shopify store, your appointments and physical inventory exist on the same platform. You can offer retail products like styling gels, conditioners, or gift cards during the booking flow or upsell them right at checkout.

### Can I set up a cancellation fee policy with my scheduling software?

Yes, modern booking systems support custom deposit and cancellation rules. You can set up policies that state deposits are non-refundable if a client cancels within 24 hours of their slot, protecting your income from last-minute calendar gaps.
